How To Play

Movement

Use WASD or the arrow keys to move around the room. On touch devices, use the joystick in the bottom-left corner.

Interaction

Walk near an object until a prompt appears, then press E (or tap the interact button) to examine it, read it, or use it.

Inventory & Journal

Press I to open your inventory and J to open your journal, which quietly records what you've discovered.

Choices

Some interactions offer choices. Pick a response with the mouse or a tap. Some choices matter more than they seem to.

Objective

You woke up in this room with no memory of how you got here. Explore. Pay attention. Find out what's happening — and decide what to do about it — before the night runs out.

Pause

Press ESC at any time to pause.
